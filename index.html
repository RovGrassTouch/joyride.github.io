Sonic Clockwork is a classic Sonic fangame that takes place a few months after the good ending of Sonic CD. Sonic Development began in 2023 as a solo project before turning into a team of passionate people years later.

A few months after the events of Little planet and Westside Island, Sonic and Tails, our protagonists, spot Dr. Robotnik and his machines going the Temporal Isles, home to Never Lake. When they both land, Amy gives them a warm-welcome; but before any of them can get too comfortable, Metal Sonic and Dr. Robotnik ambush all three of them. In the aftermath of the battle, Dr. Robotnik kidnaps Amy and flys off to his Interstellar-esque rocket. He plans on using his rocket to get to Little planet and use the Time stones (magical time-warping crystals) to rule the world. Now, Sonic and Tails have to stop Dr. Robotniks plan and save Amy.
