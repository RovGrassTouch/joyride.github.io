WELCOME

Sonic Clockwork is a classic Sonic fangame that takes place a few months after the good ending of Sonic CD. Sonic Development began in 2023 as a solo project before turning into a team of passionate people years later.

note: The demo doesn't represent the state of the game today.

STORY

A few months after the events of Little Planet, Sonic, our protagonist, spots Dr Robotnik and his machines heading to the Temporal Isles, home to Never Lake. When Sonic lands, Amy gives them a warm welcome, but before they can get too comfortable, Metal Sonic and Dr Robotnik ambush all three of them. After the battle, Dr Robotnik kidnaps Amy and flies off to his Interstellar-esque rocket. He plans on using his rocket to get to Little Planet and use the Time Stones (magical time-warping crystals) to rule the world. Now, Sonic and Tails have to stop Dr Robotnik's plan and save Amy.
